Xinyu Shan

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences (SIMIS). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the School of Data Science, Fudan University, and my M.S. in Computational Mathematics from the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Lanzhou University.

My research focuses on numerical linear algebra and scientific computing, with particular interests in eigenvalue algorithms, structure-preserving algorithms, and randomized numerical linear algebra.

Email: xyshan@simis.cn
Affiliation: Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences, Shanghai, China
More info: CV, ORCID, GitHub

Research Interests

Eigenvalue Problems. Structure-preserving and large-scale iterative methods, including LOBPCG, subspace iteration, and related projection techniques.

Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra. Randomized projection, randomized coordinate descent, Kaczmarz-type methods, and their applications to matrix equations and matrix decompositions.

Scientific Computing. Accurate, stable, and efficient numerical algorithms for structured matrix computations.
